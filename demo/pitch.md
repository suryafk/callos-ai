# Pitch (45–60 s) — draft

**Problem:** Hundreds of millions of people use basic phones or have low digital literacy. AI assistants assume an app, a data plan and a screen.

**CallOS AI:** One phone number. Ask anything. Get authorized things done. Call from any phone, ask in Hindi or English, and turn the conversation into real actions — like a wholesale order — without opening an app.

**Differentiator:** Ask → Act → Revise → Approve → Verify in one call. The voice agent (Bolna) talks; our backend enforces prices, approvals, versioning and idempotency, so the AI can't invent totals or execute a cancelled order.

**Who:** small wholesalers and shopkeepers first; later professionals (email, calendar, documents).

**Honest disclosure:** catalog, customers and orders are mocked; demo user is pre-authorized; state is in-memory.
